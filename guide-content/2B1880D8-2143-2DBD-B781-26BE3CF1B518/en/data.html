Information Collection and Use

To maintain app stability, analyze crashes and performance issues, determine device compatibility, and improve service quality, the app and its integrated Sentry SDK may process the following information after you agree to this policy:

  • Installed software list: A list of apps installed on the device, including app names, package identifiers, and version numbers. The app automatically reads this information through operating system app-information interfaces solely to identify software environments that may cause crashes, runtime errors, or compatibility issues. It is not used for advertising or user profiling.
  • Device MAC address: The MAC address of a device network interface. The app automatically reads this information through operating system device or network-information interfaces solely for device identification, runtime environment security analysis, troubleshooting, and preventing duplicate diagnostic records. It is not used for advertising or user profiling.
  • List of specific sensor types: Information about specific types of sensors supported by the device, including sensor type, name, manufacturer, and version. The app automatically reads the sensor list through operating system sensor interfaces. This does not cause the app to continuously read sensor measurements. The information is used solely to determine device feature compatibility and troubleshoot crashes and performance issues.
  • Diagnostic and usage data: Crash logs, error information, performance data, app launch and restart counts, and runtime environment information such as device model, operating system, and app version. This data is collected automatically through app logs and the Sentry SDK solely for troubleshooting, performance analysis, and service improvement.

Collection timing and method: The information above is collected only after you agree to this policy. It may be collected automatically by the app or the Sentry SDK through operating system interfaces when the app starts, when you use a relevant feature, or when a diagnostic event such as a crash or slowdown occurs. It is transmitted for processing over an encrypted network connection. We do not collect this information before you agree to this policy or process it beyond the purposes and scope expressly described here.

You may decline this policy, in which case the app will not initiate the collection described above. You may also withdraw your consent by contacting us as described under “Contact Us.” Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing performed with your consent before its withdrawal.
