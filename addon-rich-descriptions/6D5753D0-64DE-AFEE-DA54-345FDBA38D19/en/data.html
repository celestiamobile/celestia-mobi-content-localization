Rosalind Franklin, also known as the ExoMars rover, is a European astrobiology mission intended to explore Oxia Planum by 2030. It was originally planned to launch on 2022 with Russia's Kazachok lander, but it was cancelled following the ongoing Ukrainian conflict. It is now planned to be delivered onto the Martian surface with a new lander, targeting launch by 2028

Note

The Rosalind Franklin rover is only visible from 2030-Nov-01 onwards
