Cassini was a joint NASA/ESA planetary flagship mission that extensively studied Saturn and its moons in orbit. It was launched in October 1997 and reached Saturn in July 2004. Seven months upon arriving on Saturn, Huygens became the first spacecraft to land on the surface of Titan on January 2005. The mission continued studying the ringed planet until it was eventually deorbited in 2017.
