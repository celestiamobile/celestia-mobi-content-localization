The Swift Boost Mission (LINK) is a robotic spacecraft designed to boost the orbit of the Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory back to its operational altitude. Launched on 3 July 2026, it was originally meant to represent a new capability for the space industry and the United States. Multiple spacecraft failures caused Katalyst to abandon the mission on August 2026, and was subsequently deorbited a month later

Note

A series of XYZV trajectories were included to both simulate its planned rendezvous with Swift that was cancelled in August 2026, and its subsequent orbital decay on September 2026
