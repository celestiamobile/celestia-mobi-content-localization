The Solar Wind Magnetosphere Ionosphere Link Explorer (SMILE) is a joint Chinese-European mission that aims to observe the Earth's magnetosphere in ultraviolet and x-ray light. Launched in May 2026, it is designed to expand our understanding between the interaction of solar wind with our planet's magnetic fields.
