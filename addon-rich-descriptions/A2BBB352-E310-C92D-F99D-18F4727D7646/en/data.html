Sputnik 1 was the first spacecraft ever put into orbit around Earth. Launched by the Soviet Union in 4 October 1957, it sent radio signals back to Earth for 22 days before it went silent. It reentered the Earth's atmosphere on 4 January 1958

Note

Two miniaturized mockups that had been deployed from the Mir Space Station in 1997 and 1999, Sputnik 40 and Sputnik 99, were also included
