668643 (2012 DR₃₀) is a large centaur and trans-Neptunian object with a highly eccentric orbit that brings it to the inner regions of the Oort cloud. Measuring about 185–188 km in diameter, it has the largest semi-major axis of any known minor planet that is not a comet. It is discovered from the Purple Mountain Observatory in China on 6 February 2008
