The Soviet Vega program were a series of twin spacecraft that simultaneously explored both Venus and 1P/Halley. Part of an international fleet called the Halley Armada, it twice delivered a lander and a balloon onto Venus before exploring Halley, the latter of which eventually helped Giotto to attempt a close flyby of its nucleus. As of 2026, they were the last missions to successfully land on Venus
