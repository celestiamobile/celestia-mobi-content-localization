The Uranus Orbiter and Probe (UOP) is a NASA mission concept intended to study Uranus and its moons. It was originally planned to launch by 2031, allowing a Jupiter flyby on 2035 and arrival at Uranus by 2044. However, shortage in plutonium production caused a significant delay to the mission to the mid to late 2030s.

Note

  • This addon reflects the (2nd-best) interplanetary trajectory and spacecraft model described in the 2021 Decadal Survey report.
  • It also includes a vibe-coded extended mission phase where it completes 72 flybys of Uranus' moons.
