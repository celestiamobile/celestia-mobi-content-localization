This add-on adds XYZV trajectories in the period 1957-2099 for the two Martian moons, Phobos and Deimos, allowing for more precise orbit simulations, particularly during spacecraft flybys such as Mars Express, MAVEN, Hera and MMX. Additionally, it includes high resolution models for both moons and topographic maps as alternative textures.
