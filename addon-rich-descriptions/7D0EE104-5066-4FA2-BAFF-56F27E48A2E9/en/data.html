Hera is a European spacecraft aimed to study the Didymos-Dimorphos system in the aftermath of NASA's DART mission in September 2022. Launched in 2024, it reached the two asteroids on November 2026, aiming to measure the efficiency of the kinetic impact method for planetary defense.
