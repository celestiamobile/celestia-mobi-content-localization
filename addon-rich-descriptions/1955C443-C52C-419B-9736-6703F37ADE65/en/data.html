The Eris Explorer is a fictional Canadian spacecraft to one of the largest trans-Neptunian objects in the Solar System, Eris. A 2015 launch allowed a 36-year journey to flyby three of the outer gas giants (Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune) between 2018 and 2029, where it sent a single atmospheric probe to each planet it passed by. After reaching Eris in 2051, it became one of a few spacecraft that eventually reached interstellar space.
