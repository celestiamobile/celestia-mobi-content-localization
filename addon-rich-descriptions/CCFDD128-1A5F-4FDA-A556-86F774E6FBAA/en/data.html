The Colosseum, also known as the Flavian Amphitheatre, is a large amphitheatre built during the reign of Emperors Vespasian and Titus of Rome from 72–80 AD. At its peak, it is estimated to hold an audience of roughly 50,000–80,000 people, where it originally hosted many sports and gladiatorial events. Today, it is one of the most iconic tourist destinations not just in Rome, but to Italy as a whole.
