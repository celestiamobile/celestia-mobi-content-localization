Elektro-L, also known as the Geostationary Operational Meteorological Satellite (GOMS) is a series of Russian weather satellites placed in GEO, analogous to the Himawari, GOES and Meteosat. Five satellites were launched from Baikonur, Kazakhstan since 2011
