The Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory, also known as Explorer 84 or simply Swift, is a NASA space observatory launched in 2004 for studying gamma-ray bursts (GRBs) and monitoring their afterglow in X-ray, and UV/Visible light. Increased solar activity in 2024 had reduced Swift's orbital altitude significantly, risking an uncontrolled reentry by 2026. A rescue mission was attempted to save Swift from reentry, but it was abandoned after encountering multiple spacecraft failures.

Important

A series of XYZV trajectories were included to both simulate its planned rendezvous with LINK that was cancelled in August 2026, and its subsequent orbital decay that would eventually end up deorbiting itself sometime in late 2026 or early 2027
